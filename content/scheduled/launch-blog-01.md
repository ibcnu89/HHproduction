## FAQ

### Does it work with cursive / messy handwriting?
Yes. Gemini 2.5 Flash was trained on millions of handwritten samples. It handles 4th grade print through 12th grade cursive. If it can't read something, it flags it for you rather than guessing.

### What standards does it support?
Illinois Learning Standards (priority), Common Core Math/ELA, NGSS, and 30+ state variants. You select your state during setup.

### Can I use my own answer key / rubric?
Absolutely. Upload a photo or PDF of your answer key once → it becomes a reusable custom rubric. Teachers share rubrics in the community library.

### Is student data safe?
No student names, IDs, or PII ever touch our servers. Images are processed in-memory and deleted. We're not a data company — we're a grading tool.

### What if I only teach one subject?
Still $5.99/mo. The value is time, not subject count. One subject at 5 classes = 150 papers/week = 7.5 hours saved.
