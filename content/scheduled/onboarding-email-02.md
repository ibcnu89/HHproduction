## The Problem with "Standard" Rubrics

Your district gives you a rubric. It's... fine. But it doesn't match:
- Your specific essay prompts
- Your department's writing expectations  
- The way *you* actually grade

## The HomeworkHelper Way: Upload Once, Grade Forever

**Upload your rubric ONE TIME** (PDF, image, or typed) → HomeworkHelper learns it → Every future paper grades against *your* criteria.

### What "Your Rubric" Can Include:
- **Point breakdown per criterion** (Thesis: 5pts, Evidence: 10pts, Analysis: 10pts, Mechanics: 5pts)
- **Partial credit rules** ("Half credit for thesis attempt, full for clear arguable claim")
- **Grade-level adjustments** ("6th grade: prioritize structure over sophistication")
- **Subject-specific stuff** (Lab report sections, math work-showing, CER framework)

### Real Example: Mrs. Park's 8th Grade Science

She uploaded her **CER (Claim-Evidence-Reasoning) rubric** with 4 levels per category.

**Before:** 15 min/paper × 120 papers = 30 hours
**After:** Snap photo → 30 sec/paper × 120 = 1 hour

**The rubric is now a department asset.** Her PLC colleagues use it. Consistent grading across 4 teachers.

---

## Your 5-Minute Task Today

1. Find **one rubric** you use repeatedly (essay, lab report, project, math explanation)
2. Go to **Settings → Rubrics → Upload New**
3. Snap a photo or upload PDF
4. Name it ("Park CER v2", "Dept Essay Rubric 2024")
5. Grade one paper with it

**That's it.** Now every paper you (or your PLC) grade uses *your* standards.
