## The Batch Workflow (5 Steps, 15 Minutes)

### 1. Collect (2 min)
Students turn in papers. You have a stack.

### 2. Photograph (5 min)
- Open HomeworkHelper on phone
- Tap "Batch Grade"
- Rapid-fire photos: tap, next, tap, next...
- **Tip:** Use a phone stand ($12 Amazon) for hands-free

### 3. Review (5 min)
- App shows all 30 graded papers in a grid
- Green check = confident grade
- Yellow flag = "I should glance at this"
- Tap any to override

### 4. Export (1 min)
- Tap "Export All" → CSV downloads
- Columns: Student, Score, %, Standards, Feedback

### 5. Import (2 min)
- Open gradebook (PowerSchool, Classroom, Skyward, Infinite Campus)
- Import CSV → map columns → done
